Design addendum · v1

Things to Order — remaining screens

Everything the locked handoff didn't cover: the two permission screens the overlay depends on, the "Connect apps" update, the main list screen and its states, item edit, settings, recovery help, and the system notifications. Same tokens, type and assets as the locked pack. Orange appears once per screen.

Decisions baked in (change any and I'll update)
  1. Onboarding grows from 8 to 10 steps: Usage Access (08) and Draw over apps (09) sit between "Connect apps" and "All set" (now 10). The progress bar has 10 dots.
  2. Amazon Fresh and Flipkart Minutes stay in the list as disabled rows tagged Coming soon. Picking at least one of the four live apps enables the primary button.
  3. Tapping a pop-up chip ticks the item off the list, so widget, app and pop-up always agree.
  4. Reminder times are fixed defaults: once a day 9am · twice a day 9am and 6pm · custom every N hours, 8am to 10pm.
  5. Empty list means no pop-up and no pill when a shopping app opens.
  6. Items have a name and an optional one-line note (e.g. "2 packs"). No quantities, prices or categories.
